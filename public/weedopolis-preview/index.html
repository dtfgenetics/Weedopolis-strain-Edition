Weedopolis: Strain City Edition

Weedopolis is an original adult cannabis-themed property-trading browser game from DTF Genetics.

Build strain districts, manage Bud Bucks, upgrade properties with Grow Tents and Dispensaries, and resolve High Chance and Community Stash cards while competing around the city.
